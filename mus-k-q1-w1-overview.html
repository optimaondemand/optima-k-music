Essential Question
🤔 What will I learn about sound, rhythm, and music this year?
🎤
I Can Statements
I Can…
  • listen carefully to different sounds and music.
  • use my voice and instruments to make music.
  • show how music makes me feel through movement or words.
📖
Vocabulary Word
sound
Anything we can hear — music, voices, instruments, and the world around us!
💬
Reflection
One song or sound I already love is __________.
🔍
Notice & Wonder
I notice that I already know some songs by heart and that music can be fast or slow, loud or soft. I wonder how music can make me feel happy, calm, or excited and what my favorite part of music class will be.
